**Jessie Pelosi, Ph.D.**

<b>Principal Investigator </b>

I will be starting as an Assistant Professor in the Botany Department at the University of British Columbia in January, 2027. I am currently a USDA NIFA postdoctoral research fellow working with Dr. Katrina Dlugosch at the University of Arizona. I earned my Ph.D. in Botany from the University of Florida, and B.A. in Biological Sciences and B.S. in Environmental Geosciences from the State University of New York at Buffalo. I am a plant evolutionary biologist with a keen interest in fern genomics, biodiversity, and molecular evolution.  I use field collections, high-throughput sequencing, and bioinformatics to answer questions about evolutionary processes on the population and genomic scales with a particular emphasis on ferns as a study lineage. Outside of the lab, I enjoy spending time outdoors, hiking, traveling, and reading.
