# Join us! 

We are just starting to build the lab at UBC and officially open in January 2027. We are interested in recruiting students and researchers interested in plant evolution, plant life cycles, the evolution of biodiversity, plant genomics, bioinformatics, and adaptation, including undergraduate students, graduate students, and postdoctoral scholars. See below for the opportunities for each of these categories and contact Jessie (jessie.pelosi@gmail.com) if you are interested in joining!! 

* Undergraduate Students: 
	* Undergradute research was a pivotal part of my research journey. I have really enjoyed working with undergraduate students; typically I aim to work with students to develop an independent project that matches their interests and fits with the research in the lab. Undergraduates are encouraged to work directly with graduate students or postdoctoral mentors in the lab. 
	* In your email, please include a statement of interest (a couple of sentences about your scientific interests and what excites you about our lab) and a CV. 

* Graduate Students: 
	* I am always open to discussing graduate studies with students who are interested. Attending graduate school is a big commitment and finding the right advisor is key to your success. 
	* In your email, please include a statement of interest (broadly, what are your scientific interests and how do these fit in with the research in our lab), relevant research experience, and a CV. 
	* Funding is primarily through a combination of fellowships and TAships. 
	 
* Postdoctoral Scholars:
	* In your email, please include a statement of interest (what are your scientific interests), what project you would propose to work on in the lab, relevant research experience, your career path, and a CV. 
	* Funding for postdocs is limited to fellowships.
