# Welcome to the Pelosi Lab!

Welcome! This is the website for the Pelosi Lab in the Department of Botany at the [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/), opening January 2027!

Our work spans levels of diversity and time, from the evolution of major clades of plants across deep time to rapidly evolving populations of invasive species, to address the question: <b>**What are the evolutionary process by which patterns of biodiversity and genome diversity are generated and maintained?**</b>. We focus primarily on seed-free plants, especially the ferns, which are the second largest group of vascular plants and occupy a pivotal phylogenetic position sister to the seed plants. Ferns are a rich study system as they have important evolutionary traits, such as independent gametophyte and sporophyte life stages. The lab uses genomic frameworks and traditional botanical inquiry to answer fundamental questions about plant evolution.
